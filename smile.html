Shiksha par adhikar hai sab ka

Our mission is simple: help remove the barriers that keep children in rural communities from learning, growing and dreaming bigger.

Study Material

We distribute books, notebooks, stationery and other essential learning resources to children who may not be able to afford them.

Learning Support

Volunteers encourage children through educational activities, mentoring and community-based learning initiatives.

Awareness

We engage families and communities in conversations about the long-term value of education for every child.

Our volunteers in action

Small acts can create a brighter classroom.

Our volunteers visit communities, listen to local needs and help children access basic study resources. A notebook, a book or a few hours of encouragement can make a meaningful difference.

We believe education should not depend on where a child is born or what their family can afford.

“When we educate one child, we strengthen an entire community.”
